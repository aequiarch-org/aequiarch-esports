# Frequently Asked Questions

## General Questions

### Q: What is aequiarch-esports?
A: aequiarch-esports is a full-stack, game-agnostic esports platform designed for tournament organizing, team management, match tracking, and live leaderboards. It's built for everyone from casual communities to professional organizations.

### Q: Is it free to use?
A: Yes! The platform offers a free tier with basic features. We also have premium plans for organizations and power users with advanced features like custom branding and admin panels.

### Q: What games are supported?
A: The platform is game-agnostic, meaning it supports any competitive game. Currently, we have integrations with:
- CS2 (Counter-Strike 2)
- Valorant
- League of Legends
- Dota 2
- And more!

### Q: Can I use it for my own tournaments?
A: Absolutely! You can create tournaments for any game, set entry fees, manage brackets, and track results.

## Getting Started

### Q: How do I create an account?
A: You can create an account by:
1. Clicking "Sign Up" on the homepage
2. Using your email and password
3. Or signing in with Discord, Steam, or Google

### Q: How do I create a team?
A: To create a team:
1. Go to the "Teams" page
2. Click "Create Team"
3. Enter your team name and select your primary game
4. Invite other players to join your team

### Q: How do I join a tournament?
A: To join a tournament:
1. Browse available tournaments on the "Tournaments" page
2. Click on a tournament to view details
3. Click "Join Tournament" and select your team
4. Pay any entry fees if required

## Tournament Management

### Q: How do I create a tournament?
A: To create a tournament:
1. Go to the "Tournaments" page
2. Click "Create Tournament"
3. Fill in the details:
   - Tournament name
   - Game
   - Start/end dates
   - Entry fee (optional)
   - Maximum number of teams
4. Submit your tournament

### Q: What tournament formats are supported?
A: We support:
- Single elimination
- Double elimination
- Round robin
- Swiss system

### Q: How do I set up tournament brackets?
A: Brackets are automatically generated based on the tournament format. You can:
1. View the bracket on the tournament page
2. Manually advance teams after each match
3. Let the system handle bracket progression automatically

### Q: Can I have multiple games in one tournament?
A: Currently, each tournament is focused on a single game. However, you can create multiple tournaments for different games.

## Team Management

### Q: How do I invite players to my team?
A: To invite players:
1. Go to your team page
2. Click "Invite Players"
3. Enter the player's email or Discord username
4. Send the invitation

### Q: How do I remove a player from my team?
A: To remove a player:
1. Go to your team page
2. Find the player in the roster
3. Click "Remove Player" next to their name
4. Confirm the removal

### Q: Can I have co-captains?
A: Yes! You can assign roles to team members:
- Captain: Full team management permissions
- Member: Can join tournaments but cannot invite others

### Q: How do I change my team's name or game?
A: To update team details:
1. Go to your team page
2. Click "Edit Team"
3. Update the name, game, or other details
4. Save changes

## Match Management

### Q: How do I submit match results?
A: To submit match results:
1. Go to the tournament page
2. Find your upcoming match
3. Click "Submit Result"
4. Enter the score and winner
5. Submit the result

### Q: How are match points calculated?
A: Points are calculated based on:
- Win = 3 points
- Loss = 0 points
- Draw = 1 point

### Q: Can I upload match replays or VODs?
A: Yes! You can upload:
- Match replays (demos)
- Video on Demand (VOD) links
- Screenshots of match results

### Q: How do I report a dispute?
A: To report a dispute:
1. Go to the match page
2. Click "Report Dispute"
3. Explain the issue
4. Provide evidence (screenshots, replays)
5. Submit the dispute

## Payments & Subscriptions

### Q: How do I pay tournament entry fees?
A: Entry fees can be paid via:
- Credit/debit card (Stripe)
- PayPal
- Cryptocurrency (coming soon)

### Q: How do I set up a subscription?
A: To set up a subscription:
1. Go to your profile
2. Click "Billing"
3. Select a plan (Pro or Org)
4. Enter your payment details
5. Complete the subscription

### Q: Can I get a refund?
A: Refunds are available in certain cases:
- Tournament cancellation
- Technical issues preventing participation
- Disputed matches (with evidence)

### Q: How do I view my payment history?
A: To view payment history:
1. Go to your profile
2. Click "Billing"
3. View your transaction history
4. Download receipts if needed

## Troubleshooting

### Q: I can't log in. What should I do?
A: Try these steps:
1. Check your email and password
2. Reset your password if needed
3. Clear your browser cache
4. Try a different browser
5. Contact support if the issue persists

### Q: My tournament isn't showing up. Why?
A: Common reasons:
- The tournament hasn't started yet
- You're not logged in
- The tournament was cancelled
- There's a technical issue

### Q: How do I report a bug?
A: To report a bug:
1. Go to the "Help" section
2. Click "Report Bug"
3. Describe the issue in detail
4. Provide steps to reproduce
5. Include screenshots if possible

### Q: How do I request a feature?
A: To request a feature:
1. Go to the "Help" section
2. Click "Request Feature"
3. Describe your idea
4. Explain how it would benefit users
5. Submit your request
