# Platform Features

## Core Features

### 🏆 Tournament System
- **Full bracket logic** for single elimination, double elimination, and round robin formats
- **Auto-generated match slots** and team seeding
- **Automatic bracket advancement** after match results
- **Registration management** with open/closed states and slot counters
- **Browse all tournaments** (active, upcoming, completed)

### 👥 Team Management
- **Team creation** with captain assignment and role definitions
- **Public team profiles** showing record, roster, achievements, and history
- **Membership management** (invite, remove, update)
- **Team media wall** for VOD links, highlights, and screenshots

### ⚔️ Match Scheduling & Results
- **Automatic match scheduling** with round labels and time slots
- **Instant bracket updates** after result submission
- **Permanent match history** for teams and players
- **Match check-in system** with forfeit protection

### 📡 Live Match Experience
- **Dedicated match pages** with real-time score updates
- **Live event feed** logging key moments
- **Side-by-side team stats** during matches
- **Elapsed game timer** for live tracking

### 💬 Real-Time Chat
- **Match-specific chat rooms** for players, fans, and organizers
- **Instant message delivery** with usernames and timestamps
- **Chat moderation** tools for organizers

### 📊 Player & Team Statistics
- **Persistent stats accumulation** (wins, losses, points)
- **Win rate and ranking tracking**
- **Complete competitive record** over time
- **Profile statistics display**

### 🥇 Global Leaderboard
- **Ranked list** of teams and players by points, win rate, total wins
- **Game-specific filtering**
- **Podium display** for top 3
- **Real-time updates** after every match

### 🔔 Notifications & Alerts
- **Match time notifications**
- **Tournament registration confirmations**
- **Bracket advancement alerts**
- **Match result notifications**
- **Dashboard integration** for time-sensitive items

### 🧑‍💼 Personal Dashboard
- **Command center** showing user's teams, tournaments, matches, and results
- **Upcoming match schedule**
- **Recent results display**
- **Notification summary**

### 🎮 Game-Agnostic Design
- **Zero dependency** on specific game APIs or ecosystems
- **Universal support** for any competitive game (FPS, MOBA, battle royale, fighting games, card games)
- **Flexible game definition** during team/tournament setup

### 🌍 Built for Every Level
- **Same tools** for casual players and semi-pro organizations
- **No locked features** based on experience level
- **Scalable** from casual Friday night lobbies to serious competitive circuits

### 🔓 Free & Open Source
- **All features free** (no subscription, premium tiers, or ads)
- **Open source codebase** for self-hosting and customization
- **Community-driven development**

## Advanced Features

### 🎫 Tournament Registration Flow
- **Team request system** for tournament entry
- **Organizer approval/rejection** of applications
- **Automatic seeding** after approval
- **Registration deadlines** and slot visibility

### 🌱 Free Agent System
- **Player free agent status** marking
- **Browseable free agent pool** with game and role filtering
- **Direct recruitment invites** from team captains
- **Accept/decline notifications** for free agents

### 📣 Tournament Announcements
- **Organizer posting system** for rule changes and updates
- **Participant notifications** for new announcements
- **Announcement history** visibility on tournament pages

### 🏅 Achievements & Badges
- **Milestone-based badges** (first win, 100 matches, undefeated runs)
- **Leaderboard podium recognition**
- **Profile badge display**
- **Progression tracking** beyond basic stats

### 📝 Match Dispute System
- **Time-limited dispute filing** after match results
- **Organizer review process** with evidence submission
- **Ruling and result updates** based on review
- **Dispute history tracking**

### 🎙️ Player Reputation & Reviews
- **Sportsmanship ratings** from opponents after tournaments
- **Reputation badges** for high-rated players
- **Low-reputation player flags** for organizers
- **Reputation-based tournament filtering**

### 📸 Team & Player Media Wall
- **VOD link attachment** to profiles and matches
- **Highlight clip sharing** for memorable plays
- **Screenshot galleries** for achievements
- **Media portfolio building** over time

### 🔗 Public Shareable Profiles
- **Clean public URLs** for teams and players (aequiarch.gg/teams/aether-vanguard)
- **No-login-required viewing** for verification
- **Social media integration** for sharing
- **Organizer verification tool** for legitimacy checks

### 🗺️ Regional Brackets
- **Region-scoped tournaments** (NA, EU, SEA, etc.)
- **Regional leaderboards** and filtering
- **Player region selection** on signup
- **Ping-optimized competition** grouping

### 📆 Recurring Tournaments
- **Weekly/monthly/seasonal series scheduling**
- **Auto-creation of next instance** when current closes
- **Subscription system** for teams
- **Auto-reminders** for recurring events

### 🤝 Sponsorship & Prize Submission
- **Prize pool listing** by organizers
- **Detailed prize descriptions** (cash, gift cards, in-game items, hardware)
- **Prize distribution tracking** per winner
- **Visibility for competitors** on rewards

### 🛡️ Anti-Smurfing & Eligibility Rules
- **Custom eligibility requirements** (minimum rank, max win rate, account age)
- **Registration blocking** for ineligible players
- **Fair competition** through skill-appropriate grouping
- **Organizer-defined criteria**

### 📬 In-Platform Messaging
- **Direct messaging** between players and captains
- **Bulk messaging** from organizers to participants
- **Competition communication** centralization
- **Message history and search**

### 📈 Tournament Analytics for Organizers
- **Registration rate tracking** over time
- **Match duration analysis**
- **Participant activity metrics**
- **Viewer count statistics** for live matches
- **Dropout rate monitoring**

### 🔁 Sub & Stand-In System
- **Temporary stand-in registration** from approved pool
- **Captain-driven sub assignment** for unavailable players
- **Stand-in flagging** on match pages
- **Forfeit prevention** through player availability

### 🏗️ Custom Tournament Rules Builder
- **Organizer-defined rulesets** (map pool, game mode, scoring, overtime)
- **Public rule visibility** on tournament pages
- **Acknowledgment requirement** on registration
- **Rule enforcement** during matches

### 🌐 Multi-Language Support
- **Multiple language UI** availability
- **Player language preference** settings
- **Multi-language announcements** for tournaments
- **Localized content** display

### 🔐 Role-Based Access Control
- **Platform roles**: Player, Team Captain, Tournament Organizer, Platform Admin
- **Permission-based access** for each role
- **Team isolation** (captains can't modify other teams)
- **Tournament ownership** (organizers manage only their tournaments)
- **Admin oversight** with full platform control
