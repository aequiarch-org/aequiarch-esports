## Risks & Mitigations

### Technical Risks
- **Database Scaling:** Use Supabase's auto-scaling
- **Performance:** Implement caching and CDNs
- **Security:** Regular audits and penetration testing

### Business Risks
- **Competition:** Focus on niche features
- **User Adoption:** Strong marketing and community building
- **Revenue:** Diversify income sources (subscriptions, ads, sponsorships)
